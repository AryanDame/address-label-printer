# Address Label Printer

Print addresses on self-adhesive label sheet 32091 (70 × 41 mm, 3 across and 7 down on A4). Click the label you want on a picture of the sheet, check the enlarged preview, and print the address in exactly the right place. After printing, the label is marked as used and the next free one is chosen for you. Your sheet is kept in your browser, so you can finish it another day.

**Open it here: https://aryandame.github.io/address-label-printer/**

Use Edge or Chrome on a computer. In the print window choose paper size A4, Scale 100% (not Fit to page), Margins Default, and switch Headers and footers off.

The page is one self-contained HTML file. Your sheet and the address you are typing are saved in your own browser on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere.
