# Address Label Printer

Print addresses on self-adhesive label sheet 32091 (70 × 41 mm, 3 across and 7 down on A4). Click the label you want on a picture of the sheet, check the enlarged preview, and print the address in exactly the right place. Long addresses shrink by themselves so every line stays on the label, and a status line shows the printed text size. You can make the first line bold, centre the text, choose the font and the largest size, and put the last line in capitals. After printing, the label is marked as used and the next free one is chosen for you. Your sheet is kept in your browser, so you can finish it another day.

**Open it here: https://aryandame.github.io/address-label-printer/**

Use Edge or Chrome on a computer. In the print window choose paper size A4, Scale 100% (not Fit to page), Margins Default, and switch Headers and footers off.

The page is one self-contained HTML file. Your sheet, the address you are typing and your text options are saved in your own browser on this computer; nothing is sent anywhere. A sheet saved by the previous version carries over automatically.
